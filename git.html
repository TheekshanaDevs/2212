Difficult Concepts, Simplified

Quick mental models without confusing academic theory.

Lec 01 & 02

Recognizers vs Library Readers

Traditional ML is like showing a child 100 apples so they spot an apple. Deep Learning shows millions so they identify the breed. The Transformer makes the child read every book ever written—so they understand context and can write poems or recipes.

Lec 04

Zero-Shot vs Few-Shot

Zero-Shot is asking someone to do a task with zero sample answers. Few-Shot shows 2–3 sample inputs and outputs first, teaching the exact tone, style, and structure you want.

Lec 05

Chain-of-Thought (CoT)

Asking AI for an instant final answer leads to hasty guesses. Asking it to "think step-by-step" acts like a math scratchpad—it solves intermediate steps out loud, yielding far higher accuracy.

Lec 12

Chatbot vs AI Agent

A Chatbot only talks and produces text. An AI Agent is a "Smart Doer" that can access your tools, trigger APIs, schedule meetings, and execute tasks on your behalf.
